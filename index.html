Can you trade it? Tested: no edge

Backtest run on 7 Oct 2026 over 429 coins (up to a year of daily candles each): buy at the next open after a daily close above the 30-day high, long-only. Exits: the map's target with a stop at mid-range, or a trailing exit on a close below the 10-day low. Filters tried: bull phase, tight range before the breakout, breakout-day volume ≥ 1.5× its 20-day average. Costs 0.3% per round trip. Without the five biggest trades (one coin, LAB/USDT, rose about 51×), every rule lost money on average, and buying on random days with the same exits did about as well or better in most cases. The map is for finding levels to watch, not a trading strategy on its own.

How it is calculated

  • Phase (bull / bear / sideways, the cycle model from the Sarmaaya video): bull = close above the 50-day average, which is above the 200-day average and rising; bear = the mirror image; everything else is sideways, "after decline" (possible accumulation) or "after rise" (possible distribution). Under 200 days of history uses a 100-day average.
  • Breakout = a daily close (UTC) above the previous 30-day high or below the previous 30-day low. Those two prices are the triggers.
  • Date window: middle 50% (25th–75th percentile) of how much longer this coin's past quiet stretches lasted once they had lasted as long as the current one; wide window = middle 80%. Too little history → all coins pooled.
  • Direction: bull → up, bear → down, sideways → whichever half of the range price is in.
  • Afterwards: measured move (range height projected beyond the trigger, log scale) and the median days past breakouts took to reach it.
  • Accuracy test: the method re-run as of 60 days ago with only data up to then, scored against what happened.
  • Data: Bitget public API (candles, prices, volume), Binance prices for the cross-check, CoinGecko for market cap. Stablecoins and tokenized stocks are excluded. The open coin streams live from Bitget's WebSocket feed and all other prices refresh every 10 seconds. Every 30 minutes the analysis re-runs with today's still-forming candle (phase, breakout status, squeeze, windows, marked provisional); trigger, target and stop prices stay on the last 30 closed days until the 00:00 UTC close, so they match the Telegram bot.
